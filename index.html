How it works

From a message to a keepsake.

We make the process clear, collaborative and personal at every step.

01

Tell us the story

Message us on WhatsApp with the occasion, names, dates and photographs you love. A voice note works just as well.

02

We design it around them

Our studio builds the piece from a blank page — the cover, layout, palette and wording. Nothing is dropped into a generic template.

03

You approve the proof

You see the design before anything is printed, poured or packed, and we refine it with you until it feels right.

04

Crafted and delivered

We finish it by hand, pack it gift-ready and arrange delivery, with the digital surprise ready behind the scan.

A note from the founder

Sumit Laxman Jagadale

Founder · Digital Gift Wala

Digital Gift Wala began with a simple thought: the most meaningful gifts are the ones that tell a story. Today, that idea reaches beyond personalised digital gifts and occasion magazines into QR memory cards, customised hampers, corporate gifting and handcrafted keepsakes.

We also help businesses build their digital presence through PVC digital business visiting cards, websites, mobile apps, Meta Ads and practical digital solutions. Whether we are creating a gift for a special person or building a digital experience for a growing business, our focus remains the same: thoughtful design, useful technology and details that make every experience feel personal.

Our vision is to keep growing Digital Gift Wala into a place where gifting, creativity and digital innovation come together — with room for new products and services as our customers’ needs evolve.
